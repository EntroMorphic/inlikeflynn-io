Long-term commitment: the version validated today is the version that runs in production

A frozen-binary architecture holds everything still: the model never drifts, updates never churn, and no vendor-side change triggers a revalidation cycle. The classifier validated today is the classifier that runs in five years — byte-for-byte identical, with the same locked threshold and the same audit trail.

Flynn’s long-term support commitment matches that architectural property. Security maintenance, compliance documentation, and direct support extend under the terms specified at license — for the life of the deployment.

We support what we ship, for as long as you run it.
