The Flynn Roadmap

From neuron,
to nervous system.

Flynn ships in stages. Each stage stands on its own and composes into the next. Atomic equipment intelligence composes into unit, facility, and enterprise-scale systemic-awareness.

Stage 01
Today · Shipping

The neuron.One signal in. One score out.

The Flynn detector — the subject of the whitepaper — is the foundational unit. It ships today as embedded firmware, validated across five domains and ready to integrate into production equipment.

One detector · one signal · one decision
Footprint
8,480 bytes. Fits on existing MCUs.
Form
Compact binary, compiled for your target. Deterministic. Verifiable.
Runtime
Bare-metal. No OS, no network, no filesystem. Zero heap after init.
Calibration
Self-calibrating from 1,700 samples†. Threshold then locked.
Determinism
Bit-identical output across runs and silicon.
Validation
Five distinct signal domains. Same source. Same flags.
Availability
Today. Under evaluation, OEM, and enterprise license.

† Samples required for complete enrollment will vary by equipment type, state, and operational envelope.

The constant

Structural integrity at scale.

Flynn is composable — from a single detector on one asset to coordinated intelligence across a facility. As it is scaled, one principle holds at every layer: the layers above can decide which signals deserve attention, but they can never reach down and change a detector’s enrollment data. The detector you validate today stays exactly the same, no matter the depth of composition or how the equipment degrades.

Stage 02
In development · Pilots engaging 2026

The deterministic, auditable, neural network.Coordinate detectors per asset.

A coordination layer that composes multiple Flynn detectors on one asset into a single view — three to ten detectors across different sensors, with a fabric node that holds operational-regime memory and supplies cross-sensor context.

3–10 detectors · one asset · regime-aware coordination
Topology
3–10 detectors per asset. Multi-sensor coverage.
Coordination
Fabric node maintains operational-regime memory. Cross-sensor context.
Authority
Detector layer remains authoritative on what is anomalous.
Adds
Which deviations are real faults vs. regime-related. What the asset's current operating state actually is.
Separation
Coordination layer can bias attention. It cannot modify what underlying detectors classify.
Status
In active engineering. Pilot deployments engaging in 2026.
